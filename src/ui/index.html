Sign in to Microsoft Teams

Use the same account you use for Teams. Sortinghat can only do what you can already do by hand — it never stores your password.

A Microsoft sign-in window will open. It sometimes appears behind this browser window — check your taskbar if you do not see it.

What Sortinghat needs to work
  • You must be an owner of the team you want to change.
  • Private channels can only contain people who are already in the team. Sortinghat adds them to the team for you when they are missing.
  • Changes can take a minute or two to appear in the Teams app. That is Teams, not Sortinghat — refresh the channel to see them.
